“Walk me through a set you assembled.”

Begin with your scope. Then show the drawing sequence, a coordination issue, the revision you made, and the final submission or construction outcome. Be clear about what others did.

“How did you work remotely with a US team?”

Give a specific example from Florida or Seattle. Explain how you handled comments, version control, time zones, and questions you could not resolve alone.

“What would you do when the design and build requirements conflict?”

Use a real project. State the constraint, options, the person who decided, and how you revised the drawings.

“How current is your Revit production work?”

Separate hands-on Revit drafting from visualization. Show a recent sheet or model you personally edited. If another tool was used, say so.
